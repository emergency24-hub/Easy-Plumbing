EASYPLUMBING AND DRAINAGE LTD — WEBSITE

OPEN LOCALLY
Extract the ZIP completely, then double-click index.html. No installation,
build step, internet connection or local server is needed. Phone and email
links open your device's configured calling/email application.

EDIT
Company details, service descriptions, service images, areas and FAQs are
grouped at the top of script.js in the SITE object. Use a plain text editor.
The main page wording and name-based logo are in index.html.
Colours, spacing and responsive layouts are in style.css.
Keep images inside assets/ and use relative paths.

PUBLISH
Upload index.html, style.css, script.js and assets/ together to the web root
for easy-plumbing.co.uk. Configure HTTPS with your hosting provider.
Service views use URL hashes, so no server rewrite rules are needed.

CONTENT
No reviews, opening hours, response-time promises, qualifications or
accreditation claims have been added. The stock folder's Gas Safe logo is
not used because no registration details were supplied. Confirm the scope
and competence requirements for any water-heater work before advertising
specific gas or electrical tasks. Stock photographs illustrate services;
the website does not describe them as the company's completed projects.
No analytics, tracking cookies or backend are included. Review the privacy
wording against your actual information-handling practices before publishing.
